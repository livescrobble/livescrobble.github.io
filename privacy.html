Live Scrobble

Privacy policy

Live Scrobble identifies music playing around you and records it to your Last.fm account. This policy explains what the app processes and where it goes.

Last updated November 17, 2026

Summary

  • The developer does not run any servers and does not receive your data.
  • The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Audio is not recorded or stored.
  • Your listening history goes to Last.fm, because that is what the app is for.

Microphone and Shazam

When you start listening, the app uses the microphone to identify music. The app uses Apple's ShazamKit. ShazamKit turns the sound into a signature, a short fingerprint that can't be turned back into audio, and sends it to Apple's Shazam service to find a match. The app does not record, save or upload raw audio.

Apple handles these signatures under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The microphone is only used while listening is on. Listening continues in the background until you stop it in the app.

Last.fm

To scrobble, you log in to your Last.fm account. The app sends Last.fm:

  • your Last.fm username and password at login, to get a session key
  • the songs you listen to: artist, track title and the time you listened
  • the song playing right now, as soon as it is identified, so your Last.fm profile can show it as "now playing"

Last.fm handles this data under its own privacy policy: last.fm/legal/privacy.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone only:

  • Last.fm session key, in the iOS Keychain. Logging out deletes it.
  • Last.fm username and settings (listening mode, concert artist), in the app's settings storage.
  • Pending scrobbles: songs identified but not yet sent to Last.fm, for example when you are offline. They are removed once sent.

App Store and TestFlight builds do not write a log file. Development builds keep a local debug log on the device. It never leaves the device.

Deleting the app removes all of this data. The Keychain session key can stay on the device after deletion, as with any iOS app. It only works with your Last.fm account, and you can revoke it in your Last.fm settings under Applications.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not contact any server run by the developer.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
