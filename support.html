Support

Questions, bug reports, and songs that keep getting missed all go to the same place.

Writing in

Email support@livescrobble.app. Write and I'll see it.

For a bug, say what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Your iPhone model and iOS version help too. Both are in Settings → General → About.

Common questions

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and a free Last.fm account.

Why does it use so much battery?

To keep identifying music while the phone is locked, the app keeps the microphone on until you stop it. Stop listening when you leave.

Listening paused on its own.

Another app took the microphone, for example during a call. The Live Activity says so.

I was offline. Did I lose scrobbles?

No. Songs identified while offline wait on the phone and are sent to Last.fm once you're back online.

A song keeps getting missed.

Email the artist and track, and roughly where you heard it.

How do I disconnect Last.fm?

Log out in the app, which deletes the session key. You can also revoke the app in your Last.fm settings under Applications.
