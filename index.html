Live Scrobble

Scrobble the music playing around you.

Live Scrobble listens through your iPhone's microphone, names the song with Apple's ShazamKit, and adds it to your Last.fm profile. It works in a cafe, next to a radio, or at a gig.

How it works

Tap once, then put the phone away

  • 01Log in to Last.fmEnter your Last.fm username and password once. The app trades them for a session key and keeps that in the iOS Keychain.
  • 02Tap to listenThe app listens through the microphone and asks Shazam what is playing. When it gets a match, the song shows as now playing on your Last.fm profile.
  • 03Let the song playOnce the same song has been matched across at least 45 seconds, it is scrobbled. A quick snippet between tracks doesn't count.
  • 04Lock the phoneListening keeps going in the background. The current song and your count for the session sit in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Two modes

Recordings and live sets behave differently

Pick a mode before you start. It stays fixed until you stop listening.

Cafe / Radio

For recorded music playing nearby. Shazam matches the exact recording, so the app knows how far into the song it is and stamps the scrobble with when the song started.

Concert

For live sets. Live versions match less often through crowd noise, so gaps of up to 5 minutes still count as one song. Type who's playing and only their songs scrobble, not the music between sets.

Offline

No signal, no lost scrobbles

If a scrobble can't be sent, because you're underground or Last.fm is down, it waits in a queue on the phone. The queue is saved to disk, so it survives the app closing. The app keeps retrying and sends everything once it gets through. The count of songs waiting shows next to your scrobble list.

Your data

Audio stays on the phone

The app never records or saves audio. ShazamKit turns the sound into a signature, a short fingerprint that can't be turned back into audio, and sends that to Apple's Shazam service for a match. Your Last.fm login and the songs you scrobble go to Last.fm, because that is what the app is for.

There are no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The developer runs no servers and receives none of your data.

Good to know

Battery and the microphone

To keep identifying music while the phone is locked, the app keeps the microphone on until you stop it. That uses more battery than a normal app. Stop listening when you leave.

If another app takes the microphone, for example during a call, listening pauses and the Live Activity says so.
